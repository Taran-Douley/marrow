Marrow

Two games built on Marrow, a kit that makes browser games playable with one switch, by sound, by sight and by touch, and proves every level can be finished before anyone plays it.

How to play either game: press any key, click, tap or gamepad button. That is the only control. Choices are read out one at a time: press when you hear the one you want. Escape comes back to the menu. Headphones help.

Ghastly Moves

A maths rhythm game. Three beeps, then press on the fourth beat to jump the numbers that fit the rule. Seven songs, flips, number gates and bosses.

Séance Escape

Escape a haunted house by sound. Each way out is read from where it lies; a phantom listens for you. Find three relics, ring the bell, run.

Hello, Marrow

The smallest Marrow game: four lines of code, with setup, menus, every sense and a proved level.

Screen-reader users: turn off the built-in voice in each game. Every line is still written for your screen reader. Marrow helps games work for more players; it does not make them "fully accessible", and it does not replace testing with disabled players. Feedback is very welcome.
