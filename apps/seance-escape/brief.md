# Game brief

Marrow reads this to suggest accessibility features that fit the game. Change any line; the plan follows.

Name: Séance Escape
Genre: maze
Theme: haunted
Controls: one press, scanning
Engine: browser
Players: anyone, first of all players who play by sound
Features:
- a house of named rooms to explore
- a phantom that listens and hunts
- relics to find, then a ritual and a chase
- wardrobes to hide in
- Mabel, a ghost who talks and gives hints
- whispers that tell the story
- a 3D view lit by torches
