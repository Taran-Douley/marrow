Séance Escape test build

A hundred years ago a séance went wrong in this house, and something answered. Find three relics, each humming in its own room, then ring the great bell in the Front Hall to banish the phantom and escape. You walk by yourself, room to room. Each room has its own sound, so you can find your way by ear. In a room, the ways out are read one by one, each from the direction it lies: press any key, click, tap or gamepad button when you hear the one you want. Nothing is timed while you choose. The phantom cannot see: it listens. If it hears you, hide in a wardrobe, or get far away. Mabel, a friendly ghost, will point the way if you ask. The first press opens a sound guide that plays every sound that matters. Everything is also spoken and written for screen readers and braille displays. Headphones help a lot.
