Ghastly Moves test build

A ghost with a lantern walks the road through Grimsby Hollow, a Halloween story in seven places. Each place has its own kind of number rule and ends with a boss. Beat the boss to win an item for your lantern. Each gravestone has a number, spoken and shown, and three beeps. If the number fits the rule, jump on the fourth beat: press any key, click, tap, or press a gamepad button. If it is not, do nothing and let it pass. A mistake tells you why. Right answers in a row climb a tune and light the lantern. Escape comes back to the start. Everything you see is also spoken and written for screen readers.
